This website is designed without analytics, advertising, contact forms,
or embedded third-party services. Fonts and mathematical rendering assets
are served with the website.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. For information about GitHub's
processing of visitor data, see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
